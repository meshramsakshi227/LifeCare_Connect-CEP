Family privacy:
- Removed whole-home medical/medicine visibility from Family Dashboard.
- No separate Permitted Resident Dashboard.
- Family must enter a resident's complete name manually to request access.
- Resident names are not displayed to Family before approval.
- Admin receives resident-specific permission requests in Notifications.
- Each resident requires separate Admin approval.
- Only one approved resident is visible at a time.
- New approval replaces the previous permitted resident.
- After approval, Family can view only that resident's medical needs, results,
  disease history, report analysis, and booking information.
- General Family access remains limited to Dashboard, Ashram Information,
  Monthly Seminars, Timetable, Booking Notifications, and Permitted Resident.

Other application modules were left unchanged.
